# User guide
1. **Browse** — Services tab: search by name/description or filter All/Sofa/Leather/Chairs/Auto. Tap a service for details. Prices are "from" estimates in rand (e.g. R1 200), not quotations.
2. **Create an account** — Profile ▸ Sign in ▸ *Create an account*. Use a password of 8+ characters with an uppercase letter and a number. Open the confirmation email, then tap *I've confirmed my email*. Or tap **Continue with Google**.
3. **Book** — Open a service ▸ *Book this service* (or Home ▸ Book). Choose a date (tomorrow or later), an hourly time 08:00–16:00, check your address, optionally describe the job, tick the agreement and submit. Note your reference (e.g. GAU-ABC123). The request is saved on your phone as *Pending*; it is not sent to the business and the time is a preference, not guaranteed.
4. **Track and cancel** — Bookings tab: *Upcoming* and *Past*. Open a booking to see all details. Pending bookings can be cancelled (confirm the dialog).
5. **Notifications** — Bell icon: unread dot/count, tap to mark read, *Mark all read*, bin icon to delete.
6. **Settings** — Profile ▸ Settings: edit name, phone, address; notification preferences; theme (System/Light/Dark); change password (email accounts only).
7. **Help / About / Contact** — Numbered guide and FAQs under Help. Contact saves your message on this phone only.
8. **Log out** — Profile ▸ Log out. Your data stays privately on the phone for your account.
